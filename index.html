How to Play

  1. Fill the empty cells using numbers 1 to 9.
  2. Every row must contain numbers without repetition.
  3. Every column must contain numbers without repetition.
  4. Every 3×3 box must contain numbers without repetition.
  5. Use Notes to remember possible answers.
  6. Complete the puzzle to win!
